Flexible eating patterns

Patterns are tools, not tests.

There is no single perfect way to eat. These patterns can offer ideas, but personal needs and cultural food traditions matter.

{# ============================================ PATTERN GRID ============================================ #}
{# ---- Pattern 1: Mediterranean-style ---- #}
🌊

Mediterranean-style

Often emphasizes vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, nuts, olive oil, fish, and shared meals. It can be adapted to many cuisines.

{# ---- Pattern 2: DASH-style ---- #}
❤️

DASH-style

Centers fruit, vegetables, whole grains, beans, lean proteins, and lower-sodium choices. Medical goals should be discussed with a clinician.

{# ---- Pattern 3: Plant-forward ---- #}
🌱

Plant-forward

Adds more beans, lentils, tofu, grains, nuts, seeds, fruit, and vegetables without requiring one strict definition.

{# ---- Pattern 4: Your Household Pattern ---- #}
🏡

Your household pattern

The most useful approach respects your culture, schedule, access, budget, preferences, and health needs.

{# ============================================ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER ============================================ #}
⚕️

Before making a major change

Talk with a doctor or registered dietitian if you manage a health condition, take medication, are pregnant, have food allergies, or need individualized support.
