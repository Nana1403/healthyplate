Nutrition basics

Different nutrients, different roles

You do not need to track every nutrient to eat well. Variety across meals helps cover your bases.

{# --- Right: Nutrient Definitions --- #}
Protein
Supports growth, repair, and many everyday body processes.
Carbohydrates
The body's preferred source of energy, found in fruit, grains, beans, and more.
Fats
Support cells, hormones, and absorption of certain vitamins.
Fiber
Supports digestion and is naturally present in many plant foods.
